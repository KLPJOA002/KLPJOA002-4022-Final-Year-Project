\subsection{Biological Activity}
\label{subsec:ch1.section1.subsec1}
%----------------------------------------------------
Sand prawn activity has a large effect on the surrounding ecosystem. Pillay and Branch \cite{inbook} explain that by physically burrowing into the sediment, they distribute buried sediment to the surface, alter the surface structure via the introduced mounds, which can increase surface area, and alter both chemical and structural properties of the sediment through burrowing and nutrient mixing. Venter et al \cite{venterWaterFiltrationBurrowing2020} further shows that sand prawn bi-directional water pumping filters can reduce phytoplankton biomass by up to 70\% in estuarine environments which can help with ecosystem resilience.
%The microtopography of sediment surface in inter-coastal regions is strongly linked to the bio-diversity (cite) and bio-density within the benthic ecosystem. Specifically, sand prawn population within the Langebaan lagoon are strong members in the bioturbation industry and play a large part in moving sediment around, which alters the physical structure of the sediment, and the chemical properties therein. Measuring and analysing the microtopography presents itself as a strong measurement for the bio-density and activity of sand prawns in these regions. 

\subsection{Technical application}
Schenone et al \cite{schenoneSedimentMicrotopographyPredicts2024} demonstrates that surface microtopography can be used as a reliable metric to predict biochemical processes. \begin{align}
\nabla \cdot \bm{E} &= \frac{\rho}{\epsilon_0}, \\
\nabla \cdot \bm{B} &= 0, \\
\nabla \times \bm{E} &= -\frac{\partial \bm{B}}{\partial t}, \\
