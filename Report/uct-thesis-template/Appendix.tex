Factual corrections
	
Minimum range. The draft said the D456’s minimum operating distance is 600 mm. Your Chapter 3 derives Z\_min ≈ 0.51 m and cites the datasheet value of 0.52 m, so I changed it to “about 0.52 m at 1280×720”. I also added the 687 mm tilt anomaly from Chapter 8 as the practical limit.
D456 range. “Optimised for longer range” is now tied to the 95 mm baseline and cited, instead of being stated without support.
	
TODOs resolved
	
Shorter-range cameras. I filled these in from sources already in your bib:
D415: about 160 mm minimum (Carfagni), and best short-range reconstructions (Servi).
D435: under 1 cm error within 1 m (Rustler).
	
Content added
	
Chapters 1 and 8 both say certain items would be “discussed in Chapter 10”, but they were missing from the draft. I added them:
	
Feature detection. Automated mound and burrow detection, which was removed from scope (Chapter 1.4).
Preset with fusion. Testing High Accuracy combined with fusion (Chapter 8.1).
	
I also carried over the limitations listed in Chapters 8 and 9:
	
single trials per condition, so repeatability is unknown;
the ± values on the sand data reflect processing options, not repeat captures;
no field deployment;
specular noise on wet sand.
	
Other additions:
	
Lighting drift. Quantified with your auto-exposure data (5300 down to 1700–2050, 15:40–17:35).
